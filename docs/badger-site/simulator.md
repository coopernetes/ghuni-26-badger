##  Edit apps on your badge No badge connected
Use desktop Chrome or Edge.
- Connect the badge to your computer with a USB data cable. Quickly press RESET twice. Wait for USB Disk Mode on the screen and the BADGER drive on your computer.
- Select Connect badge. Choose the BADGER drive itself and allow access.
- To edit an installed app, select Choose badge app. To install your current project, leave it open in the editor.
- Edit the code, then select Upload or Install. Wait for confirmation.
- Select Disconnect, then eject BADGER from your computer. Wait for the drive to disappear before unplugging. If the badge does not restart on its own, press RESET. Open the app on the badge to test it.
